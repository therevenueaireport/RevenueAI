# Account Expansion Toolkit: Value Map, Whitespace Grid, Triggers, and Plays

**Use this file when:**
- You are building or refreshing the quarterly plan for a top account and need the sheets, not just the prompt.
- An account is healthy but flat and nobody can say why it has not grown.
- A seller wants to "pitch the full suite" and you need a test for which paths are real expansion.
- You are preparing a QBR and want the expansion conversation grounded in value the customer already sees.

The SKILL.md holds the logic. This file holds the instruments. Run them in the order below.

---

## 1. The Planning Sequence

| Step | What you do | Output | Time (rule of thumb) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pull the account data pack | Data pack (Section 2) | 10-15 min |
| 2 | Build the value map | What they own, use, and get value from (Section 3) | 10 min |
| 3 | Run the readiness gate | Go, or fix-first list | 5 min |
| 4 | Fill the whitespace grid | Candidate paths (Section 5) | 10 min |
| 5 | Score paths; keep two | Two paths with reasons | 10 min |
| 6 | Map roles and relationship strength per path | Roles table | 10 min |
| 7 | Attach a trigger to each path | Trigger and date | 5 min |
| 8 | Write three plays with owners and first steps | Play list | 10 min |
| 9 | Write the failure risk for each path | Risk lines | 5 min |
| 10 | Review at the next QBR and update | Version 2 | At QBR |

## 2. Step 1: The Account Data Pack

| What | Where (system category) | How far back | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contract: products, quantities, price, term, renewal date | Billing system or CRM contract records | Current contract plus prior | Discounts that would anchor an expansion price |
| Usage: active users vs licensed, feature adoption, trend | Product analytics or customer success platform | 6-12 months, monthly | Seat count looks fine but core feature is unused |
| Value evidence: outcomes the customer has stated or measured | QBR decks, success plans, call recorder, case study drafts | 12 months | Value your team claims that the customer never said |
| Open problems | Support tickets, escalations, open bugs, health score notes | 90 days | Severity-one issues and repeat tickets |
| Contacts and relationship strength | CRM contacts and activity history, meeting history | 12 months | One-threaded accounts; champions who left |
| Org structure | Customer-shared org charts, professional social network, the account's own job postings | Current | Guesses dressed as facts. Mark each box confirmed or inferred |
| Stated goals | Kickoff notes, QBRs, executive sponsor emails, the account's public reports | Current year | Goals your team wrote for them |
| External events | Account press releases, earnings or funding news, leadership changes | 6 months | Events with no link to your product |

---

## 3. Step 2: The Value Map

One row per product, module, or use case they own.

| Product / use case | Team using it | Licensed | Active (last 30 days) | Value status | Evidence (customer's words or metric, source) | Open problems |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Value status levels

| Status | Descriptor | Expansion implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven** | Customer has stated or measured an outcome, in their words or their numbers | Expansion can build on this |
| **Used, unproven** | Active usage, but nobody has named an outcome | Prove it first; a value readout is the play |
| **Underused** | Active use well below licensed (rule of thumb: under 70 percent of seats active, or the core feature unused) | Fix adoption before asking for more |
| **Broken** | Open problem blocking value (integration down, severe tickets, sponsor unhappy) | Fix first (SKILL.md rule). No expansion ask |

---

## 4. Step 3: The Readiness Gate

All must be "yes" before any expansion ask. A "no" turns that item into Play 1.

| Check | Yes looks like |
|---|---|
| At least one product or team is at "Proven" | A customer-stated outcome you can quote |
| No "Broken" rows | No open severity-one issues, no unresolved escalations |
| Executive sponsor is known and reachable | Met in the last two quarters |
| Champion is still in seat | Confirmed in the last 90 days |
| Renewal is not at risk | Health score and CSM read agree |

**Rule of thumb:** if the renewal is inside 90 days and anything above is "no," secure the renewal first and fold expansion into the renewal conversation only if the customer raises it.

## 5. Step 4: The Whitespace Grid

Rows are the account's teams, regions, or business units. Columns are your products or use cases.

| Cell code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** | Owned and proven |
| **U** | Owned, used, unproven |
| **L** | Owned, low use |
| **A** | Not owned; adjacent to a proven cell (same product new team, or new product that extends a proven outcome) |
| **N** | Not owned; no link to proven value. This is a new sale (SKILL.md rule) |
| **?** | Unknown. Org knowledge is missing |

### Path types

| Path type | Pattern in the grid | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Spread** | Same product, new team or region next to a P cell | NA team proven; EMEA team on spreadsheets |
| **Deepen** | New product or module for a team with a P cell, extending the same outcome | Team proven on call summaries; add coaching module for the same managers |
| **Scale** | More volume, seats, or a usage tier where a P cell is hitting a ceiling | Seat overage three months running |
| **Upgrade** | Higher tier unlocks something a P team has asked for | Repeated feature requests that the next tier includes |

**More than two "?" cells in a row:** the plan is guessy (SKILL.md limitation). Play 1 becomes an org-mapping conversation with the champion.

### Path scoring (score each A cell or upgrade candidate 0-2)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extends proven value | No link | Link through a U cell | Directly next to a P cell |
| Tied to a stated goal | No goal | Inferred goal | Goal the customer stated, with source |
| Access to the buyer | Nobody known | Known, no relationship | Known and has met us, or champion will introduce |
| Trigger present | None | Expected but undated | Dated trigger in the next two quarters |
| Size worth the effort | Small relative to current contract | Meaningful | Material to the account and your number |

| Total (rule of thumb) | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 8-10 | Plan it now |
| 5-7 | Plan it; fix the weakest criterion with the first play |
| 0-4 | Park. Revisit next quarter |

**Keep the top two.** The SKILL.md asks for two credible paths, not a list.

## 6. Steps 6-7: Roles and Triggers

### Roles per path

| Role | Who (name or "unknown") | Relationship | What they need to believe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer for the new spend | | Strong / known / unknown | |
| Existing champion who can vouch | | | |
| Leader of the new team or use | | | |
| Likely blocker (procurement, IT, a rival team, a competing tool's owner) | | | |
| Coach inside the account | | | |

### Trigger bank

| Trigger type | Examples | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| Account planning cycle | Annual or regional planning, budget lock dates | Ask the champion; kickoff notes |
| Leadership change | New leader in the target team | Professional social network, account announcements |
| Reorg or merger | Teams combined; acquired company brought in | Press releases, champion |
| Goal milestone | Mid-year review of the stated goal | QBR, sponsor emails |
| Your usage data | Seat ceiling hit, new-team logins, feature requests | Product analytics |
| Renewal | Contract renewal window | Billing system |
| Competing tool contract end | The tool the new team uses comes up for renewal | Champion, procurement |

**No trigger, no play (SKILL.md rule).** An undated "sometime this year" is not a trigger.

## 7. Step 8: The Plays Library

| Play | When to use | First step | Typical owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value readout | Proven value exists but the new buyer has not seen it | Build a one-page outcome summary in the customer's numbers | CSM |
| Fix-first | A Broken or Underused row | Name the issue, owner, and date in writing to the sponsor | CSM + support |
| Champion introduction | New team leader unknown or cold | Ask the champion for a specific introduction, with a one-line reason they can forward | Account executive |
| Peer proof | The new team doubts it fits them | Short call between the proven team's user and the new team's lead | CSM |
| Scoped pilot | New team wants evidence before committing | Write success criteria before starting; time-box to 30-60 days | Account executive |
| Sponsor alignment | Path depends on a goal the sponsor owns | Put the goal and the path on the next sponsor meeting agenda | Account executive |
| QBR agenda item | Path needs a forum | Add "progress on [stated goal]" with the path as an option, not a pitch | CSM |

## 9. Worked Example: Acme and Customer Z

*Illustrative only. Acme is a fictional 400-person B2B software company selling a support-conversation analytics platform. Customer Z is a fictional 2,000-person logistics company. All figures are invented.*

### Value today

| Product / use | Team | Active / licensed | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ticket analytics | NA support (120 agents) | 110 / 120 | Proven | Support VP at last QBR: "Repeat contacts are down a fifth since we started tagging root causes." QBR deck |
| Reporting connector to their data warehouse | NA support ops | n/a | Broken | Sync failing since August; ticket open 41 days |
| Coaching module | NA support managers | 3 / 12 | Underused | Managers say they have no time; product analytics |

### Readiness gate

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| A proven row | Yes (ticket analytics) |
| No broken rows | **No** (connector) |
| Sponsor known and reachable | Yes (Support VP, met last quarter) |
| Champion in seat | Yes (NA support ops lead) |
| Renewal not at risk | Yes, renewal in eight months |

**Result:** gate fails on one item. Play 1 is the connector fix. No expansion ask until it closes.

### Whitespace grid

| Team | Ticket analytics | Coaching | Product feedback module |
|---|---|---|---|
| NA support | P | L | A |
| EMEA support (60 agents, older tool) | A | N | N |
| Product management | N | N | A |
| Sales | ? | N | ? |

### Path scoring

| Candidate | Extends value | Stated goal | Access | Trigger | Size | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EMEA support rollout (Spread) | 2 | 2 ("one support model across regions," sponsor email) | 1 (EMEA director known, never met) | 2 (EMEA tool contract ends in Q2) | 2 | 9 |
| Product feedback module for product team (Deepen) | 2 (same root-cause tags) | 1 (inferred from their public roadmap post) | 2 (champion works with the product lead weekly) | 1 (planning cycle expected, undated) | 1 | 7 |
| Sales team access | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1. Park: new sale |
| Coaching module growth | n/a | | | | | Not a path. Underused row; fix adoption |

**Reasoning:** EMEA scores highest because it extends the proven outcome to a team with the same job, ties to a goal the sponsor wrote down, and has a dated trigger. The product feedback path extends the same data but the goal is inferred, so the first play must confirm it. Sales has no link to proven value: that is a new sale, so it is parked and listed as such.

### Paths

**Path 1: EMEA support rollout.** Roles: Support VP (economic buyer, strong), NA ops lead (champion), EMEA support director (new team lead, unknown), procurement (blocker on timing). Trigger: EMEA tool contract ends Q2; decision needed by end of Q1. **Failure risk:** the EMEA director was not part of the original decision and may see this as an NA tool imposed on them.

**Path 2: Product feedback module.** Roles: Head of product (economic buyer, unknown), NA ops lead (champion, introducer), product ops manager (user lead). Trigger: product planning cycle, date to confirm. **Failure risk:** product may already have a feedback tool and see this as overlap.

### First three plays

| # | Play | Path | Owner | First step | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fix-first | Both | CSM + support engineering lead | Written fix plan for the connector sent to Support VP | This week |
| 2 | Peer proof | Path 1 | CSM | Ask NA ops lead to host a 30-minute call with the EMEA director on root-cause tagging results | After connector fix, within three weeks |
| 3 | Champion introduction | Path 2 | Account executive | Ask NA ops lead to introduce the head of product, and confirm the planning date in that call | Within six weeks |

## 10. Decision Table and Red Flags

| If you see | It means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| No row at "Proven" | You have usage, not value | Run a value readout before planning expansion |
| Expansion path relies on a team you have never met | Guessy org knowledge | First play is an introduction, not a pitch |
| Every path is a new product to the same buyer | Selling everything (Quick prompt warns against it) | Check for a Spread path first |
| Champion left in the last quarter | The plan rests on a gap | Re-establish a champion before any path |
| Seat overage for three months, nobody has called | Scale path being missed | Quick Scale conversation with the admin |
| Sponsor's stated goal changed | Paths may be tied to an old goal | Re-score triggers and goal links |
| Plan has five paths | No prioritization | Keep two. Park the rest with a reason |

## 11. AI Assist: Splitting the Work

| Task | AI assistant | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Summarize usage exports, tickets, and QBR notes into the value map | Yes | Confirms value status against what the customer actually said |
| Fill the whitespace grid | Drafts from pasted org data | Marks every inferred box as "?" unless confirmed |
| Score paths | Drafts | Owns access and trigger scores |
| Write plays | Drafts | Assigns owners who agreed |
| Politics, procurement, sponsor sentiment | Cannot know (SKILL.md limitation) | Adds from relationship knowledge |

### Follow-up prompts after the Quick prompt

1. "Before proposing paths, build a value map: product, team, active vs licensed, value status (proven, used unproven, underused, broken), and the evidence quoted from my input. If I gave no customer-stated outcome for a row, it is not proven."
2. "List any open value problems. If there are any, make fixing them play 1 and do not propose an expansion ask until it is closed."
3. "Build a whitespace grid of teams by products. Code each cell P, U, L, A, N, or ? using my input only. Mark anything you inferred about the org as ?."
4. "Score each candidate path 0 to 2 on: extends proven value, tied to a stated goal, access to the buyer, dated trigger, size. Keep the top two and list the rest as parked with a reason."
5. "For each path, write the honest failure risk in one plain sentence. Do not soften it."
6. "List every fact in this plan that did not come from my input, including names, team sizes, dates, and goals."

### Errors this job invites, and how to check

| AI error | How to spot it | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Invents org structure or role names | Teams or titles you did not supply | Run follow-up prompt 6; mark as ? |
| Proposes every product | More than two paths, or paths with no value link | SKILL.md rule: new sale, not expansion |
| Generic triggers | "When budget allows," "next year" | Require a dated event |
| Soft failure risks | "May require alignment" | Ask: "What specifically would make them say no?" |
| Treats usage as value | "High usage, so proven" | Proven requires the customer's words or numbers |
| Skips the fix-first step | Broken row present, Play 1 is a pitch | Run follow-up prompt 2 |

## 12. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to spot it | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Planning from the rep's memory | No data pack, no sources | Pull Section 2 first |
| Pitching expansion to an unhappy customer | Open escalations at the time of the ask | Readiness gate |
| One-threaded plan | Only the champion appears in every role | Map at least the buyer and the new team lead |
| Plays with no owner or date | "Team to follow up" | Name one person and a date |
| Never revisited | Same plan at two QBRs | Review at the next QBR (SKILL.md step 5) |
| Calling a new sale expansion | Path to a team with no link to proven value | Move to Parked as a new-sale opportunity |

## 13. The 5-Minute Version

1. Write what they own and the one outcome they have said out loud.
2. If anything is broken, that is the plan this quarter. Stop.
3. Name the nearest team that does the same job without you. That is path 1.
4. Name the event and date that makes now the time. No date, no play.
5. Write one play with an owner and a first step, and one sentence on why it might fail.

## 14. The Deep Version

For top-ten accounts, multi-year expansion targets, or accounts in an executive sponsor program.

| Addition | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Org map confirmed with the champion, box by box | Replaces guessed structure |
| Value readout in the customer's own numbers, signed off by their sponsor | Proof the new buyer will believe |
| Interview two users in the target team before proposing | Tests whether the adjacent need is real |
| Path model: expected value by path, with timing and probability stated as assumptions | Prioritizes across accounts |
| Joint success plan with the customer for path 1 | Turns the plan into a shared document |
| Monthly check of triggers from usage data and news | Timing catches up with reality |
