# Test Whether an AI Category Will Last: Toolkit

**Use this file when:**
- A vendor is offering a discount for a two- or three-year term in a category that did not exist a few years ago.
- Your CRM, call recorder, or productivity suite vendor just announced something that overlaps a tool you pay for.
- Two tools in your stack are converging on the same job and renewal for one of them is coming up.
- Finance asks "will we still need this in eighteen months?" and you need a defensible answer.

The SKILL.md holds the logic: the five category questions of RENEW, a framework from The Revenue AI Report ([framework](https://www.therevenueaireport.com/frameworks/renew)), and the decision rules. RENEW stands for R: replace or amplify, E: existing intent, N: new work, E: economics unlocked, W: where does the human go. RENEW is directional: it shows where the product sits on each axis and what has to be true for it to still exist in eighteen months. This file holds the instruments: the scoring sheet, the absorption-pressure check, the verdict rules, contract terms by verdict, diligence questions, and a worked test.

---

## 1. The Test Sequence

| Step | What you do | Output | Time (rule of thumb) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Write the tool, category, contract term, and annual value | Header | 5 min |
| 2 | Map what your own platforms already do in this area | Platform overlap table | 10 min |
| 3 | Draw the workflow and mark which steps the tool owns | Workflow ownership map | 5 min |
| 4 | Score the five category questions | Category score (0-10) | 10 min |
| 5 | Score absorption pressure | Pressure score (0-8) | 5 min |
| 6 | Read the verdict from the grid | Durable / contested / absorbable | 2 min |
| 7 | Pick contract terms and a watch signal | Terms and signal with owner | 8 min |

## 2. Inputs Checklist

| Input | Where to get it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What your platforms ship today in this area | Admin consoles, release notes you have received, your platform account team in writing | Overlap must be evidence, not guesswork |
| What your platforms have announced (public, in writing) | Published release notes, announcements | Announced is evidence; rumored is not |
| The workflow, step by step, and who does each step | Process owner | Shows step vs. whole-workflow ownership |
| Where the tool stores outputs and history | Vendor documentation, your admin | Drives switching cost |
| Contract term, price, renewal mechanics, exit terms | Order form draft | What you can still change |
| Current usage, if renewing | Tool admin log | Usage among assigned users, not volunteers |
| What happens to the work if the vendor disappears | Process owner | The manual fallback and its cost |

**Evidence rule:** every overlap claim cites a source you could show procurement. "I heard they are building it" is recorded as unknown.

## 3. Category Score: The Five RENEW Questions

Score each 0, 1, or 2. Write one line of evidence per score.

| RENEW question | 0 (absorbable side) | 1 (mixed) | 2 (durable side) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R: Replace or amplify | Removes the person from the step; the pitch is fewer people | Removes some steps, leaves the person owning the outcome | Makes the person measurably more capable; the person stays accountable |
| E: Existing intent | Buyers do not search for or budget for this category; vendor creates the need | Buyers have the problem but budget sits elsewhere | Buyers already search, budget, and ask for the category by its job |
| N: New work | Same work, done faster | Faster work plus some new output | Work that was not done at all before, because it was not feasible |
| E: Economics unlocked | Cost reduction only | Cost reduction that enables a modest new use | Makes something previously unaffordable affordable, so new buyers or uses appear |
| W: Where the human goes | Nowhere; no human decision remains | Human reviews outputs | Human holds a clear decision the tool feeds, and the tool gets better with their judgment |

**Category score = sum, 0 to 10.**

---

## 4. Absorption Pressure Score

RENEW reads the category. These checks, from the Quick prompt, add what RENEW does not cover: how exposed the tool is to the platforms you already own. They are this toolkit's addition, not part of RENEW.

| Check | 0 (low pressure) | 1 | 2 (high pressure) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform overlap today | None of your platforms ships anything similar | A platform ships a weak or partial version | A platform ships a comparable version you already pay for |
| Announced overlap | Nothing public | Public announcement, not shipped | Shipped to some customers, or in your contract |
| Workflow ownership | Owns the whole workflow end to end | Owns several steps | Owns one step inside a workflow a platform owns |
| Data gravity | Tool holds unique data you cannot get elsewhere | Tool holds derived data you could rebuild | Tool reads data entirely held by your platforms |

**Pressure score = sum, 0 to 8.** Per the SKILL decision rule: if a platform already ships a weak version, assume a stronger version arrives within a year. Score "platform overlap today" at least 1 in that case.

## 5. Verdict Grid (rule of thumb)

| | Pressure 0-2 | Pressure 3-5 | Pressure 6-8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Category 8-10** | Durable | Durable, watch closely | Contested |
| **Category 5-7** | Durable, watch closely | Contested | Absorbable |
| **Category 0-4** | Contested | Absorbable | Absorbable |

**Override rules:**
- A tool can be split. If one capability scores absorbable and another durable, give a verdict per capability, then a verdict for the contract (the contract follows the capability you are actually buying it for).
- Unknown evidence on a pressure check scores 1, not 0. Uncertainty is risk.
- The grid prices risk. An absorbable verdict can still justify a one-year purchase if the payback lands inside the year.

### The four positions

These come from the RENEW amplification axis: the R question (amplify or replace) crossed with the N question (new or old work). Amplification plus new work is the durable corner.

| Position | Description | Buying stance |
|---|---|---|
| New work that amplifies a person | Most defensible | Longer term acceptable with exit terms |
| Old work that amplifies a person | Real value, heavily contested; margin erodes as the capability commoditizes | Short term, price protection |
| Old work that replaces a person | Feature, not category | Month-to-month or one year, buy only on payback |
| New work that replaces a person | Rare, high risk, thin evidence | Pilot with kill criteria before any term |

## 6. Contract Terms by Verdict

Per the SKILL decision rules: contested verdicts get short contracts, not discounts; switching cost is a risk to price, not a reason to sign longer.

| Term | Durable | Contested | Absorbable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | Up to multi-year, with annual exit for non-performance | One year with renewal option | One year or less; monthly if available |
| Price protection | Renewal cap | Renewal cap, written | Not worth trading term for |
| Data export | Full export of inputs, outputs, history in a standard format | Same, within a fixed number of days on request | Same, tested before signature |
| Derived data | Returned or deleted on termination, in contract | Same | Same |
| Termination for overlap | Not needed | Right to exit or downgrade if your platform ships comparable functionality | Same, plus no auto-renewal |
| Seat flexibility | Annual true-up | Quarterly reduction right | Reduction right at any time |
| Model change notice | Notice before material model changes | Same | Same |

**Discount trap:** a larger discount for a longer term on a contested tool is the vendor pricing in your switching cost. Decline the term, not the tool.

## 7. Watch Signals

Pick one per verdict. Assign an owner and a review date.

| Signal | What it would change |
|---|---|
| Your platform ships the specific capability you bought the tool for | Contested moves toward absorbable; trigger exit review |
| The vendor moves from owning a step to owning the full workflow | Pressure falls; contested may move to durable |
| Usage among assigned users falls two quarters in a row | Durability does not matter if nobody uses it |
| The vendor's pricing shifts toward per-outcome from per-seat | Often a sign the seat is evaporating; re-run "where does the human go" |
| Your team starts doing the work in a general AI assistant instead | Absorption by a different route; re-score |
| The vendor is acquired or changes models without notice | Re-run the full test |

## 8. Diligence Questions (ask in writing)

| Area | Question | A weak answer looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Overlap | "What do you do that our CRM and call recorder will not do in a year?" | A list of features, not a workflow |
| Workflow | "Which steps of this workflow do you own end to end, and which do you hand back?" | "We integrate with everything" |
| Human | "Which decision does the person make with your output that they could not make before?" | "They save time" |
| Economics | "What can a customer do with you that was unaffordable before?" | Only cost savings |
| Data | "What happens to our data, outputs, and derived data on termination?" | Points to an FAQ, not the contract |
| Model | "What happens to our workflows when you change models?" | No notice process |
| Quality | "Show us three outputs you consider bad, and what you did about them." | Changes the subject |
| Churn | "Which customers turned this off, and why? Anonymized is fine." | "Nobody has" |

## 10. Worked Example (illustrative)

**Company:** Acme, a 400-person B2B software company. **Tool:** an AI proposal and security-questionnaire response tool. **Proposed contract:** three years, with a larger discount than the one-year price. **Capability bought for:** answering security questionnaires and RFPs from an approved answer library.

**Platform overlap.** Acme's CRM ships AI email drafting (source: admin console). Its document suite ships a general AI writing assistant (source: in use today). Neither ships a governed answer library with approval workflow. Nothing announced in writing. One manager "heard" the CRM vendor is building proposal features: recorded as unknown.

**Workflow ownership.** Intake questionnaire, match to approved answers, draft, route to subject experts for approval, track answer freshness, export. The tool owns all six steps.

**Category score.**

| Question | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| R: Replace or amplify | 2 | Solutions engineers still own final answers; tool makes each one cover far more questionnaires |
| E: Existing intent | 2 | Buyers already budget for questionnaire response; Acme's own team asked for it |
| N: New work | 1 | Mostly faster old work; answer-freshness tracking is new |
| E: Economics unlocked | 1 | Lets Acme respond to smaller deals' questionnaires it used to decline |
| W: Where the human goes | 2 | Subject experts approve and own the answer library |

Category score: 8.

**Absorption pressure.**

| Check | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Platform overlap today | 1 | General writing assistant can draft answers, without governance |
| Announced overlap | 1 | Unknown, scored 1 per the override rule |
| Workflow ownership | 0 | Owns the full workflow |
| Data gravity | 1 | Answer library is Acme's content; rebuildable but costly |

Pressure score: 3.

**Verdict:** Durable, watch closely (Category 8, Pressure 3).

**Reasoning.** The drafting step alone would be absorbable: the general writing assistant already drafts. What Acme is buying is the governed library and approval routing, which no platform it owns ships. The unknown rumor is not evidence and does not change the score, but it sets the watch signal.

**Contract terms.** Decline three years. Accept two years with annual exit for non-performance, a renewal price cap, full export of the answer library and approval history within 30 days on request, and a model-change notice clause. The discount difference was smaller than the cost of being locked in if the watch signal trips.

**Watch signal.** The CRM or document suite ships governed answer libraries with approval workflow. Owner: RevOps lead. Review: each quarter before the renewal window.

**Next action.** Procurement sends the revised terms. Owner: Head of RevOps. By: end of next week.

## 11. AI Assist

### Split the work

| Task | AI assistant | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Structure the test and draft per-question evidence | Yes, from what you paste | Correct anything not from your own platforms |
| Platform overlap | Only from release notes and docs you paste | You confirm what you actually own and have switched on |
| Vendor roadmap claims | Never | Record as unknown unless written and public |
| Verdict | Draft | You decide, using the grid |
| Contract terms | Draft clauses | Procurement and legal review |

### Follow-up prompts (use after the Quick prompt)

1. "Score each of the five questions 0-2 with one line of evidence each. Use only the information I gave you. Where you lack evidence, say UNKNOWN."
2. "Here is what our platforms ship today and what they have announced in writing: [paste]. Score absorption pressure 0-2 on overlap today, announced overlap, workflow ownership, and data gravity."
3. "Split the tool into its capabilities. Give a verdict for each, then say which capability the contract is really for."
4. "List every statement in your analysis about any vendor's future plans. Remove each one or mark it as speculation."
5. "Draft contract clauses for: renewal price cap, data and derived-data export on request, right to exit if our platform ships comparable functionality, model-change notice."

### Check the AI's output for these errors

| Error this job invites | How to catch it |
|---|---|
| Invented roadmap claims ("the CRM vendor plans to...") | Prompt 4; delete anything without a written public source |
| Market-wide reasoning instead of your platforms | Every overlap line must name one of your platforms |
| Treating cost reduction as unlocked economics | Ask: "who becomes a buyer or what becomes possible that was not before?" |
| Recommending a discount for term on a contested verdict | Contested gets short terms, per the SKILL rule |
| Single verdict for a multi-capability tool | Prompt 3 |

## 12. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to spot it | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Signing longer to lower price | Discount justifies the term in the approval memo | Price the switching cost; shorten the term |
| Judging the category, not your stack | No mention of what your platforms do | Fill the platform overlap table first |
| Rumor as evidence | "We heard" in the evidence column | Record as unknown, score 1 |
| Ignoring the human question | Pitch is about fewer people | Re-score replace or amplify honestly |
| Exit path written after signature | No export or overlap clause in the order form | Write the exit before signing |
| Watch signal with no owner | Signal listed, nobody checks it | Assign owner and a review date |

## 13. Two Versions

### 5-minute version
1. What do our own platforms already ship here? (one line per platform)
2. Does the tool own the whole workflow or one step?
3. Where does the human decision sit after the tool?
4. Verdict: if a platform ships a version and the tool owns one step, call it contested or absorbable and cap the term at one year with a data export clause.

### Deep version (multi-year, material spend, or a core workflow)
- Full platform overlap table with sources, reviewed by each platform's admin.
- Diligence questions sent in writing; answers attached.
- Per-capability verdicts.
- Manual fallback cost estimated: what it costs to do the work if the vendor vanishes.
- Contract clauses reviewed by legal; data export tested before signature.
- Watch signal on the quarterly renewal calendar with a named owner.
