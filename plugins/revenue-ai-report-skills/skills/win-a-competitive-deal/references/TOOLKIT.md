# Win a Late-Stage Competitive Deal: Toolkit

**Use this file when:**
- A deal is in the final round, a named competitor is still in it, and there is a decision date.
- The deal sits in commit and the evidence behind that is a rep's confidence.
- Someone is about to offer a discount and nobody has confirmed what the buyer is deciding on.
- A manager or executive is about to join the deal and needs a one-page read first.

The SKILL.md holds the logic. This file holds the instruments: the evidence pull, the criteria ledger, the honest scorecard, the risk ranking, the move menu, the confirming question bank, and a worked deal plan. The plan ends in three actions: two owned moves and one confirming question.

---

## 1. The 30-Minute Deal Session

| Minutes | What you do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Pull the evidence (Section 2) | Buyer quotes with names and dates |
| 5-10 | Build the criteria ledger: stated vs. assumed | Criteria ledger (Section 3) |
| 10-15 | Score each stated criterion against the competitor | Honest scorecard (Section 4) |
| 15-20 | Run the buying-group check and rank the risks | Three ranked risks (Section 5) |
| 20-25 | Pick two moves with owners and dates | Two moves (Section 6) |
| 25-30 | Write the confirming question; set the forecast call | Question and forecast category (Sections 7-8) |

---

## 2. Evidence Pull

| What | Source system | How far back | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer statements on what they are deciding on | Call recorder transcripts, buyer emails | Whole deal | Stated criteria must be in the buyer's words |
| Buyer statements about the competitor | Same | Whole deal | What they said, not what the battlecard says |
| Buying group: names, roles, last contact | CRM contact roles, calendar, email | Whole deal | Shows who is engaged and who is missing |
| Economic buyer: named, met, stated position | CRM, call notes | Whole deal | Decision rule one depends on it |
| Decision process and date as the buyer described it | Call notes, mutual plan | Whole deal | Tests the close date |
| Mutual plan, if any | Shared document | Current | Whose dates are these? |
| Pricing discussed so far | Quotes, email | Whole deal | Before any concession |
| Competitor profile or battlecard | Enablement library | Current version | Claims to test, not facts |
| Prior closed deals against this competitor | CRM | 2 quarters | Base rate; see the win-loss review skill |

**Quote rule:** every buyer statement is recorded with who said it and when. An unattributed statement is moved to the assumed column.

## 3. Criteria Ledger

| # | Criterion | Stated or assumed | Who said it (name, role) | When | Exact words | Weight to buyer (H/M/L, from their words) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | | | |

**Classification rules:**

| Situation | Classify as |
|---|---|
| Buyer named it, in their words, recently | Stated |
| Buyer named it once, early, never since | Stated, flag as possibly stale |
| Rep believes it from experience with similar buyers | Assumed |
| Appears in the competitor battlecard as "what buyers care about" | Assumed |
| Buyer agreed when the rep suggested it | Assumed until the buyer raises it unprompted |
| Procurement or security requirement in writing | Stated |

**Decision rule:** if your theory of winning rests on an assumed criterion, the deal is unqualified until the buyer says it.

## 4. Honest Scorecard

Score only stated criteria. Assumed criteria get a line below the table, unscored.

| Criterion | Weight (from ledger) | Us (1-5) | Competitor (1-5) | Evidence for our score | Evidence for theirs | Who in the buying group sees it this way |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Scoring scale:**

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer has said we fall short here |
| 2 | Likely weak; buyer has questioned it |
| 3 | Parity, or no buyer signal either way |
| 4 | Buyer has said we are stronger, or proof was accepted |
| 5 | Buyer has said this is why they lean to us, and the economic buyer agrees |

**Honesty check:** at least one criterion must favour the competitor. If none does, re-score: the buyer has a reason they are still evaluating them.

**Reading the scorecard:**

| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|
| We lead on high-weight criteria | Protect the lead: proof and executive alignment |
| We trail on one high-weight criterion | That criterion is the deal. Move 1 addresses it |
| We lead only on low-weight criteria | We are a strong second. Reframe or reweight with the economic buyer |
| Mostly 3s | We do not know enough. The confirming question matters more than any move |

## 5. Buying-Group Check and Risk Ranking

### Buying-group check

| Role | Named? | Met? | Their stated position | Will argue for us in the room? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | | | | |
| Champion | | | | |
| Technical evaluator | | | | |
| User lead | | | | |
| Procurement / legal | | | | |
| Security | | | | |

### Risk ranking

Rank by what would kill the deal fastest. Per the SKILL rules, a missing economic buyer ranks first, ahead of the competitor.

| Risk | Default rank | Kills the deal by |
|---|---|---|
| No confirmed economic buyer | 1 | Decision made or blocked by someone you never met |
| No champion who will argue for you in the room | 2 | Nobody defends you when you are not there |
| Trail on a high-weight stated criterion | 3 | Buyer picks the competitor on the criterion that matters |
| Decision process or date unconfirmed | 4 | Deal slips or goes to a process you did not plan for |
| Security, legal, or procurement step not started | 5 | Late-stage block after the decision |
| Competitor has the incumbent relationship | 6 | Status quo with a known vendor wins ties |
| Theory rests on an assumed criterion | Any | You are solving the wrong problem |

Adjust ranks to this deal, but write down why if the economic buyer risk is not first.

## 6. Move Menu

Pick two. Each needs an owner and a date.

| If the top risk is | Candidate move | Typical owner |
|---|---|---|
| No economic buyer | Sponsor-to-economic-buyer meeting, with an agenda tied to the buyer's outcome | Your executive sponsor |
| Weak champion | Give the champion a written case they can forward internally, in their language | AE |
| Trail on a stated criterion | Proof on that criterion: reference call with a similar customer, technical session, written plan | AE plus solutions or services |
| Unconfirmed process or date | Walk the decision steps backward from the date with the champion; draft a mutual plan | AE |
| Security or procurement not started | Send the security pack and contract early; book the review | AE plus security lead |
| Incumbent relationship | Make the cost of standing still concrete, with the economic buyer | AE plus sponsor |
| Assumed criterion | Ask the confirming question before any other move | AE |

### Discount gate

| Question | If no |
|---|---|
| Are the decision criteria confirmed by the buyer? | No discount. A discount into unknown criteria buys nothing |
| Has the buyer said price is a deciding criterion, in their words? | No discount. Address the stated criteria |
| Is the economic buyer engaged? | No discount. You would be negotiating with someone who cannot sign |
| Is there a trade (term, timing, scope, reference) in return? | Do not discount without one |

## 7. Confirming Question Bank

One question that would confirm or kill your theory. Pick or adapt one; ask it this week.

| Theory being tested | Confirming question |
|---|---|
| Economic buyer supports us | "When [economic buyer] looks at the two options, what will they weigh most?" |
| Decision date is real | "What happens on your side if this is not decided by [date]?" |
| We lead on the top criterion | "If you had to choose on [criterion] alone today, where do we stand?" |
| Price is not the decider | "Setting price aside, is there anything that would stop you choosing us?" |
| Champion will argue for us | "When this goes to [decision forum], who will present the recommendation, and what will they say?" |
| Competitor's strength matters less | "How important is [competitor's strength] relative to [our strength] for the people who sign?" |
| The signer is who we think | "Who signs, and has anything like this gone through them before?" |

**Rule:** if the answer kills the theory, change the forecast category the same day.

---

## 8. Forecast Call

| Evidence | Category (rule of thumb) |
|---|---|
| Economic buyer engaged, champion will argue in the room, stated criteria favour us on the top weight, process and date confirmed | Commit |
| Champion in place; economic buyer named but not yet engaged, or one or two other gaps, each with a dated move to close it | Best case |
| No economic buyer identified, or no champion, or theory rests on an assumed criterion | Pipeline, regardless of stage |

Per the SKILL rule: no champion who will argue for you in the room, no commit.

## 10. Worked Example (illustrative)

**Deal:** Acme, a 400-person B2B software company, selling to a 1,200-person logistics business. Value: mid six figures annually. Stage: final round. Competitor: "Rival X." Buyer's stated decision date: end of month. Forecast: commit.

**Criteria ledger.**

| # | Criterion | Stated? | Who | Exact words | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Implementation time | Stated | Ops director (champion), last week | "We need this live before peak season, no exceptions." | H |
| 2 | Reporting depth | Stated | Analytics lead, two weeks ago | "Your reporting is the best we've seen." | M |
| 3 | Security review | Stated | Security lead, in writing | Questionnaire due before contract | H (gate) |
| 4 | Price | Assumed | Rep | Never raised by the buyer | Unscored |

**Scorecard.**

| Criterion | Weight | Us | Rival X | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implementation time | H | 2 | 4 | Champion asked twice about our timeline; Rival X quoted a shorter one in writing |
| Reporting depth | M | 5 | 3 | Analytics lead quote; demo feedback |
| Security review | H | 3 | 3 | Neither submitted yet |

Honesty check passes: Rival X leads on the highest-weight criterion.

**Buying group.** Champion: ops director, will argue for us on reporting, worried on timeline. Economic buyer: the COO, named, never met. Security lead engaged by email only.

**Ranked risks.**
1. No confirmed economic buyer. The COO decides and has only heard about us second-hand.
2. Trail on implementation time, the top stated criterion.
3. Security review not started; a late block would push past the date.

**Reasoning.** The rep's instinct was a discount to "close the gap." The discount gate fails on two lines: price was never stated, and the economic buyer is not engaged. The gap is time to value, not price. Reporting depth is a real strength, but it is medium weight; leading only there makes Acme a strong second.

**Two moves.**
1. Executive sponsor meets the COO with a dated implementation plan that shows go-live before peak season, named implementation lead attached. Owner: Acme VP Sales. Date: this Thursday.
2. Security pack submitted and review booked. Owner: AE with Acme's security lead. Date: Friday.

**Confirming question** (to the champion, before the COO meeting): "If we can show a go-live before peak season that your team believes, is there anything else that would stop the COO choosing us?" If yes, the theory is incomplete and the new criterion goes in the ledger. If no, implementation time is the deal.

**Forecast call.** Moved from commit to best case: champion in place, economic buyer named but not met, trailing on the top criterion, both gaps with dated moves. Reverts to commit only if the COO meeting happens and the confirming answer holds.

## 11. AI Assist

### Split the work

| Task | AI assistant | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Extract buyer statements from transcripts and emails with speaker and date | Yes | Spot-check that quotes are verbatim and correctly attributed |
| Classify stated vs. assumed | First pass | Rep and manager challenge every "stated" |
| Score the scorecard | Draft | You know what the buyer meant; adjust |
| Rank risks | Draft | Manager challenges the weakest assumption |
| Pick moves and owners | Suggest | You decide; owners must agree |
| Predict competitor behaviour | No | Not predictable from public material |

### Follow-up prompts (use after the Quick prompt)

1. "List every decision criterion with the exact quote, the speaker's name and role, and the date. Move any criterion without a quote to the assumed list."
2. "Find the criterion where the competitor is strongest based on what the buyer said. If you rated us ahead on everything, re-score and explain."
3. "Is there a confirmed economic buyer in this material? Quote the evidence. If not, make that risk number one."
4. "Should we discount? Answer only by checking: criteria confirmed, price stated by the buyer, economic buyer engaged, trade available."
5. "Write the single question that would most quickly prove our theory of winning wrong."

### Check the AI's output for these errors

| Error this job invites | How to catch it |
|---|---|
| Assumes the buyer prefers you | Prompt 2; the honesty check must pass |
| Rep beliefs reported as buyer criteria | Every criterion needs a quote and a name (prompt 1) |
| Paraphrased quotes presented as verbatim | Check three quotes against the transcript |
| Battlecard claims treated as fact | Battlecard content stays in "assumed" unless the buyer said it |
| Five moves instead of two | Ask it to rank and return two, linked to the top risks |
| Confident plan from thin notes | If buyer quotes are few, the first move is a discovery conversation |

## 12. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to spot it | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Discounting into unknown criteria | Concession offered before the ledger exists | Run the discount gate |
| Forecasting commit on champion enthusiasm | Champion named, economic buyer not met | Apply the forecast table |
| Arguing on your strengths, not their weights | Moves all address medium-weight criteria | Point Move 1 at the top-weight criterion you trail |
| Treating "they agreed" as "they said" | Criteria the rep suggested and the buyer nodded to | Classify as assumed |
| Never asking the confirming question | Review checklist shows it unanswered | Owner and date on the question itself |
| Late plan for an early mistake | Deal was never qualified | Say so; late-stage plans cannot fix it |

## 13. Two Versions

### 5-minute version
1. Write the buyer's top stated criterion in their words, with who said it.
2. Is the economic buyer confirmed and met? If not, that is the top risk.
3. Where does the competitor lead, per the buyer?
4. One move with an owner and a date, and one question to ask this week. Check the forecast category against the evidence.

### Deep version (large deal, executive involvement, or a concession on the table)
- Full transcript pull with every criterion quoted and attributed.
- Manager and a peer who knows the competitor score the scorecard independently, then reconcile.
- Base rate from recent closed deals against this competitor, by segment.
- Executive sponsor briefed with the one-page plan before any buyer meeting.
- Discount gate reviewed by the deal desk before any concession.
- After the decision, win or lose, log the stated criteria and outcome for the next competitive win-loss review.
