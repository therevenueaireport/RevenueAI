# Prepare a Customer QBR

> From the [Revenue AI Report skill library](https://www.therevenueaireport.com/skills). Level: Start Here · Task: Keep Customers · Setup: About 30 minutes · For: Customer Success, Sales Leader

**Outcome:** A QBR agenda that leads with the customer's results, admits what did not work, and earns the next quarter's plan.

## When to use
- Before any executive-facing QBR.
- When renewal is within two quarters and the last QBR was a product usage tour.

## Inputs you need
- The outcomes the customer bought
- Usage and results data for the quarter
- What went wrong this quarter, honestly

## Quick prompt
Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or any assistant. Replace the [bracketed] parts.

```text
You are a customer success leader preparing a quarterly business review. The customer bought to achieve: [paste outcomes]. This quarter's results: [paste usage and results]. What went wrong: [paste]. Build a 45-minute agenda: open with their results in their terms, one honest section on what did not work and what we changed, the plan for next quarter tied to their goals, and one decision we need from them. Give me the three questions most likely to come from their executive and draft answers. Do not hide the bad news in the middle.
```

## Process
1. Start from the outcomes they bought, not the features you shipped.
2. Put results first, misses second, plan third, ask last.
3. Rehearse the three hard questions.
4. Send the agenda ahead. Surprises belong in the content, not the structure.
5. Leave with one decision made, not a list of follow-ups.

## Decision rules
- No usage chart without a sentence about what it meant for their goal.
- A miss with no stated fix is a credibility loss. Never present one without the change you made.
- If you cannot name the decision you need, you are not ready for the meeting.

## Output
- A 45-minute agenda: results, misses with fixes, next-quarter plan, one decision.
- Three likely executive questions with drafted answers.

## Quality checks
- Results are in the customer's terms, not product terms.
- Every miss carries its fix.
- The ask is explicit.

## Limitations
- The AI cannot invent results you do not have. Thin data makes a thin QBR, honestly labeled.
- It does not know the politics in the room. Adjust the ask accordingly.

## Worked example
**Input:** Bought to cut forecast preparation time. Result: prep down from 6 hours to 2. Miss: the mobile rollout stalled and field reps never adopted it.

**Output:** Open: forecast prep is down two-thirds, which returned about four hours per manager per week. Miss: mobile stalled; we changed the rollout to manager-led in week six and adoption recovered to 70 percent. Next quarter: extend to the renewals team. Decision needed: sponsor names a renewals lead by end of month.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Results first, in their terms?
- [ ] Every miss paired with a fix?
- [ ] One clear decision requested?
