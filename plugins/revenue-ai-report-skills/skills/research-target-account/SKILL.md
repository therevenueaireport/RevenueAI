# Research a Target Account

> From the [Revenue AI Report skill library](https://www.therevenueaireport.com/skills). Level: Start Here · Task: Research · Setup: About 15 minutes per account · For: Sales Representative, SDR and BDR, Sales Leader, Partnerships

**Outcome:** A one-page account brief with the business context, likely priorities, and a point of view worth a reply.

## When to use
- Before a first meeting or a top-tier outbound sequence.
- When an account moves into your target list and you need a hypothesis fast.

## Inputs you need
- Company name and website
- Recent news, earnings notes, or job postings you have
- What you sell and the problem it solves

## Quick prompt
Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or any assistant. Replace the [bracketed] parts.

```text
You are an account researcher preparing a seller for a first meeting. Using only the material I paste below, write a one-page brief on [COMPANY]. Include: what the company does and how it makes money, three likely priorities this year based on the evidence, one risk or pressure they face, the roles most likely to care about [YOUR PRODUCT PROBLEM], and a two-sentence point of view I could open with. Flag anything you are guessing. Do not invent facts, numbers, or quotes. If the material does not support a section, say what is missing.

Material:
[paste news, job posts, site copy]
```

## Process
1. Collect source material: their site, recent news, job postings, annual report if public.
2. Paste the material. Ask for the brief.
3. Check every claim against the source. Delete anything the AI could not support.
4. Add one thing the AI cannot know: your last interaction, a mutual contact, a product usage signal.
5. Turn the point of view into your opening line.

## Decision rules
- No source, no claim. Unverified lines get cut, not softened.
- If the material is older than six months, label the brief as dated and verify before the meeting.
- If you cannot form a point of view, the account is not ready for a personalized touch. Use a lighter touch or wait.

## Output
- One-page brief: business, priorities, pressure, buying roles, opening point of view.
- A short list of missing facts to confirm live.

## Quality checks
- Every fact cites pasted material or is marked as an assumption.
- The point of view is about their business, not your product.
- Nothing confidential or scraped in violation of terms is included.

## Limitations
- The AI knows only what you paste plus training data that may be stale. Verify names, titles, and numbers.
- It cannot see intent signals, your CRM history, or private relationships.

## Worked example
**Input:** Company: 800-person logistics software firm. Pasted: Q2 press release about a new carrier API, five open RevOps roles, and a CEO interview about margin pressure.

**Output:** Priorities: carrier integration launch, building a RevOps function, cost control. Opening POV: companies launching a carrier API usually discover their quote-to-cash process breaks before the product does; we help revenue teams catch that early. Assumption flagged: no evidence of their current quoting stack.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Did you verify every name and number?
- [ ] Is the opening line about them?
- [ ] Did you add one thing only you know?
