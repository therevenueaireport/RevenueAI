# Measure AI ROI

> From the [Revenue AI Report skill library](https://www.therevenueaireport.com/skills). Level: Intermediate · Task: Measure Results · Setup: About 1 hour · For: Executive and Founder, Revenue Finance, Revenue Operations, Sales Leader

**Outcome:** An outcomes-first investment case that traces one strategic result through its metric driver, operating constraint, AI fit, readiness, OAR level, cost, and measured return.

## When to use
- Renewal decisions on any AI tool.
- Board or budget reviews of AI spend.
- Before expanding a pilot to more seats.

## Inputs you need
- The strategic outcome, owner, period, and pre-AI baseline
- The metric driver selected and the operating constraint beneath it
- Tasks, workflow, data, integrations, people, skills, and readiness gaps
- Full cost and before-and-after results for the original metric

## Quick prompt
Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or any assistant. Replace the [bracketed] parts.

```text
You are a skeptical finance and operating analyst reviewing an AI investment. Strategic outcome and baseline: [paste]. Metric driver and why it was selected: [paste]. Operating constraint across tasks, workflow, data, integrations, people, and skills: [paste]. AI fit, readiness gaps, OAR level, full cost, and before-and-after result: [paste]. Test the chain from spend to the original outcome. Separate activity, capacity, savings, contribution, and revenue. Do not infer a return from hours saved. List attribution limits and give a keep, expand, fix, or stop verdict with the caveat it must carry.
```

## Process
1. Name one strategic outcome, its owner, measurement period, and pre-AI baseline.
2. Decompose the outcome into metric drivers. For sales velocity, use opportunity count multiplied by average deal size and win rate, divided by sales-cycle length.
3. Choose one driver from the baseline evidence, then map its tasks, process, workflow, data, integrations, people, skills, ownership, incentives, and controls.
4. Test AI suitability separately from organizational readiness. Record every dependency that must be fixed first.
5. Choose Optimize, Amplify, or Reinvent based on the scale of operating change required.
6. Write the full cost, compare the original metric before and after, separate capacity from financial return, and produce the verdict with its attribution caveat.

## Decision rules
- Hours saved count as money only if they became measurable output. Otherwise they are capacity, and capacity is a maybe.
- If you cannot name the before number, you cannot claim ROI. Measure first, renew later.
- If AI cannot credibly change the selected operating constraint, choose another intervention rather than manufacturing an AI use case.
- Optimize, Amplify, and Reinvent are different investments. Do not report task efficiency as operating-model change.
- A tool that misses its bought outcome but helps elsewhere is still a miss on the original thesis. Say both things.

## Output
- Payback verdict from your numbers only.
- Time saved versus revenue gained, separated.
- The board-ready sentence with its caveat.

## Quality checks
- Full cost includes people's time, not just the invoice.
- Time saved is not automatically counted as revenue.
- Unmeasurable items are listed, not assumed away.

## Limitations
- Attribution is genuinely hard. This method separates what you can defend from what you cannot; it does not manufacture certainty.
- Small samples and short windows produce noisy reads. Say so when they do.

## Worked example
**Input:** AI meeting-summary tool: $48k per year plus about 4 admin hours weekly. Bought to give reps selling time back. After 6 months: reps report 3 saved hours weekly; meetings per rep unchanged; pipeline per rep up 4 percent.

**Output:** Verdict: paid back in capacity, not yet in revenue. The 3 hours did not become more meetings. Pipeline per rep is up 4 percent but cannot be attributed cleanly with this data. Board sentence: the tool returned measurable rep capacity at acceptable cost; we have not yet shown it converts to pipeline, and we will decide that at renewal with a conversion target set now.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Full cost counted, including admin hours?
- [ ] Time and revenue kept separate?
- [ ] Caveat written into the verdict?
