# Build a Board AI Update

> From the [Revenue AI Report skill library](https://www.therevenueaireport.com/skills). Level: Advanced · Task: Measure Results · Setup: About 1 hour · For: Executive and Founder, Revenue Finance

**Outcome:** A board-ready summary of AI spend, results, and failures that survives a skeptical director.

## When to use
- Quarterly board prep.
- Any time AI spend is material enough to appear in a budget line.

## Inputs you need
- Total AI spend this period and what it bought
- Measured results, including the tools that were shut off
- Next period's planned bets

## Quick prompt
Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or any assistant. Replace the [bracketed] parts.

```text
You are a board member who has seen a hundred AI slides. Here is our AI spend and results: [paste]. What we shut off and why: [paste]. Next period's bets: [paste]. Help me build the update: the one-sentence summary of what AI returned this period, the spend broken into working, unproven, and stopped, the failure we are reporting and what it taught us, and next period's bets with success criteria attached. Then give me the three hardest questions a director will ask and honest answers. No hype. If the results are thin, the slide says they are thin.
```

## Process
1. Split spend into working, unproven, and stopped.
2. Lead with the one-sentence verdict a director could repeat.
3. Report one failure with its lesson. Boards trust teams that count reversals.
4. Attach success criteria to every future bet.
5. Rehearse the three hard questions.

## Decision rules
- Every claimed return carries its measurement method in one line.
- A period with zero reported failures reads as a period with no measurement. Include the misses.
- No new bet without kill criteria attached.

## Output
- One-sentence verdict.
- Spend split: working, unproven, stopped.
- One failure with its lesson, and next bets with criteria.
- Three hard questions with honest answers.

## Quality checks
- Time saved is not presented as revenue.
- At least one failure is reported.
- Every future bet has kill criteria.

## Limitations
- The update is only as honest as your measurement. Thin measurement, thin slide.
- It cannot decide your risk appetite. That is the board's conversation, not the AI's.

## Worked example
**Input:** Spend $310k across 9 tools. Two clearly working, four unproven, three stopped including an AI SDR after deliverability damage.

**Output:** Verdict: AI returned measurable value in two of nine tools this period; we stopped three and learned faster than we spent. Failure reported: the AI SDR, killed at week seven after complaint rates crossed our threshold; lesson, kill criteria set before signing work. Next bets carry the same criteria structure.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Verdict repeatable in one sentence?
- [ ] One failure included with its lesson?
- [ ] Hard questions rehearsed?
