## Part 2: Worked example

**Acme Software evaluates an AI meeting assistant** that records calls, writes summaries, and fills CRM fields. Fictional; figures illustrative.

### Primary test and number

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Primary test | Increase revenue |
| Metric | Win rate, via better discovery data and manager coaching |
| Baseline | 20% win rate, last four quarters |
| Target and date | 23% by end of the next two quarters |

Sponsor first said "it saves reps time". Pushed for the primary test, the sponsor chose revenue, because saved time had no plan attached.

### Full cost

| Line | Year 1 | Ongoing | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seats (40 reps) | $48K | $48K | Quote |
| Implementation | $10K | $0 | Quote |
| RevOps time to map fields | 60 hours | 10 hours a quarter | Estimate |
| Manager training | 2 sessions | Quarterly refresh | Plan |

### Scores

| Test | Score | Evidence | What could go backwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reduce cost | 1 | Vendor claims admin time saved; no cost line actually stops | None |
| Increase profit | 0 | Not assessed until revenue effect is known | Seat cost grows with headcount |
| Increase revenue | 2 | Reference customer with similar ACV saw better discovery field completion; no win-rate proof yet | Reps distrust summaries and stop reading them |
| Decrease risk | 1 | Better CRM data for forecast; claim only | **Recording consent** in two regions not yet reviewed |
| Increase raving fans | 0 | No customer-facing change | Buyers uncomfortable being recorded |

### Gate

Best test at 2; no -1 yet, but the consent question could become one. **Decision: Pilot**, after legal confirms recording consent rules. Hand-off: [Set AI Pilot Kill Criteria](../../../../revenue-ai-report-skills/skills/set-ai-pilot-kill-criteria/SKILL.md).

### Pilot design that followed

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Team | 8 AEs, 1 manager |
| Pass | Discovery fields complete on most opportunities; manager uses summaries in weekly coaching; win rate on piloted deals tracked against the rest of the team |
| Kill | Any consent complaint; reps override or ignore summaries on most calls after week 4 |
| Decision date | End of quarter, fixed at the start |

### Vendor questions

1. Which customers with our deal size measured win rate before and after, and can we speak to one?
2. How is consent handled per region, and who is liable if it fails?
3. What does field mapping cost when our CRM schema changes?
