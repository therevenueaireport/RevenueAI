## Part 2: Worked example

**Acme Software, mid-market new business.** Fictional. Outcome metric: new ARR. Times are what the team measured in a two-week sample.

| Phase | Starts when | Ends when |
|---|---|---|
| Find | Account added to a rep's target list | First meeting held |
| Move | First meeting held | Proposal requested |
| Close | Proposal requested | Contract signed |

### Find

| # | Task | People | Process | Results | Friction | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Build the target list | RevOps, reps | ICP filter in CRM plus rep picks | Target list; coverage | Reps add off-ICP accounts by hand | **Fix first**: lock the ICP filter |
| F2 | Research the account | AE, skill varies | Manual web search, no standard brief | Brief; meeting rate | 40 min per account; quality varies | **O**: AI drafts a standard brief |
| F3 | Write first outreach | AE | Templates edited by hand | Replies | Generic, low reply rate | **O**: GRACE prompt from the brief |
| F4 | Follow up | AE | Sequence tool | Meetings booked | Fine | H (no change) |
| F5 | Run the first meeting | AE | Video call | Qualified opp | None | **H** |

### Move

| # | Task | People | Process | Results | Friction | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Run discovery | AE | Call plus notes | Documented problem | Notes incomplete | **O**: AI call notes into CRM fields |
| M2 | Update CRM after calls | AE | Manual entry | Pipeline accuracy | About 30 min per day per rep | **O**: auto-capture from call notes |
| M3 | Run the demo | AE, SE | Standard demo | Technical fit | Demo not tailored | H, with AI prep from M1 notes |
| M4 | Build the business case | AE | Spreadsheet, rarely done | Buyer-validated value | Skipped on most deals | **A**: AI drafts from discovery, required before proposal |
| M5 | Multi-thread the account | AE | Ad hoc | Stakeholder coverage | Single-threaded deals stall | H, with AI stakeholder map |

### Close

| # | Task | People | Process | Results | Friction | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Write the proposal | AE | Copy last proposal | Proposal | Two days on average | **O**: template plus AI draft |
| C2 | Get pricing approval | AE, manager, finance | Email chain | Approved price | Waits several days | **Fix first**: approval rules and SLA |
| C3 | Negotiate | AE, manager | Calls | Agreed terms | None | **H** |
| C4 | Legal review | Legal | Redlines by email | Signed-off terms | Long queue | **Fix first**: standard fallback clauses |
| C5 | Get signature | AE | E-sign | Closed won | None | H |

### Act now

| Task | Mark | Why now | Next step | Owner | Proof metric | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M4 Business case | A | Deals without one stall after proposal | Pilot AI-drafted business case with 5 AEs | Sales manager | Win rate on piloted deals vs baseline | End of quarter |
| C2 Pricing approval | Fix first | Days of waiting inside the deal | Write approval thresholds and a 24-hour SLA | RevOps | Days from proposal request to approved price | 30 days |
| M2 CRM update | O | Admin time and bad data feed every later step | Turn on call-note capture for one team | RevOps | Field completion rate; rep admin time | 45 days |

### What the map showed

- Two of the three slowest steps (C2, C4) were process problems no AI tool would fix.
- The highest-leverage AI step (M4) was one reps were skipping, so AI changed the workflow (Amplify), not just its speed.
- Relationship steps (F5, C3) stayed human; AI only prepares the human for them.
