# Capacity math beats hiring plans

Every planning season someone asks me how many reps we need. My answer is always the same; it depends on what the current ones spend their week doing.

Last year I shadowed four reps for a full day each. I logged every task in 15-minute blocks. Selling time averaged a little under three hours a day. The rest went to internal meetings, data cleanup, and hunting for decks.

So before we opened six new headcount, we tried something cheaper. We removed one recurring internal meeting, automated the two worst data tasks, and put the decks in one folder with a search bar.

Selling time went up by about 40 minutes a day per rep. Across a team of 22, that's close to the output of three reps.

We still hired. Just three instead of six.

Shadow one rep for one day this month. Log it honestly. You'll find your first hire hiding inside the calendar.
