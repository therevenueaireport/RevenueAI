# The forecast call nobody wants

Last Tuesday I sat in a forecast call that ran 74 minutes. We moved zero deals.

I counted. Eleven reps, 38 opportunities, and roughly four minutes of actual inspection. The rest was people reading their CRM notes out loud to a manager who could read.

So I tried something the next week. I cut the call to 30 minutes and asked each rep one question: what changed since last Friday?

Not much, it turns out. Most deals don't change in a week; they drift. And drift is invisible when you read notes aloud.

Here is what we do now:

1) Reps post the change, not the status, by Thursday noon.
2) I flag the three deals with the biggest gap between stage and evidence.
3) The call covers those three. Nothing else.

The call is 25 minutes. Our commit accuracy went from "vibes" to within 8% two quarters running (small sample; I'll keep checking).

Run the numbers on your own call. Count the minutes spent on inspection versus recitation. Then cut the recitation.
