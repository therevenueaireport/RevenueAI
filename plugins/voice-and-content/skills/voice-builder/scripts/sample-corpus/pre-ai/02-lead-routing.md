# Routing rules rot

When I inherited our routing logic, it had 212 rules. I asked who wrote rule 147. Nobody knew.

That's normal. Routing rules are like a garage; every quarter somebody adds a shelf and nobody throws anything out.

We spent two weeks mapping every rule to the outcome it was meant to protect (speed to lead, territory fairness, or capacity). About a third protected nothing we could name. We deleted them.

What happened? Speed to first touch dropped from 41 minutes to 9. Reps stopped arguing about who owned what, mostly because the rules finally made sense when you read them.

I won't pretend the audit was fun. It was a spreadsheet, a whiteboard, and a lot of coffee.

But you don't need a new tool for this. You need a list and an afternoon.

Pull your routing rules into one sheet this week. Write the outcome next to each one. If you can't write it, you've found your first deletion.
