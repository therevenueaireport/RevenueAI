# Fair territories are a myth (and that's fine)

I've redrawn territories five times in four years. None of them were fair.

That's not a confession. It's math. Accounts don't arrive in even piles; they cluster by industry, by region, by whoever bought last year.

What we can do is make the unfairness visible. Each territory gets a score: account count, total addressable spend, and historical win rate in that segment. We publish the scores. Reps can see exactly where the gaps are and argue with the inputs, not the outcome.

Arguments dropped. Not to zero (sales teams argue; it's a feature), but they moved from "this is rigged" to "your spend estimate for logistics is too high." That second argument I can fix.

Will the model survive the next reorg? Probably not in this exact form.

Publish your territory inputs before your next redraw. Let people check your math. Then run the numbers together.
