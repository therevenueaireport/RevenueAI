# We had 46 dashboards

Three years ago our team had 46 dashboards. I know because I built 19 of them.

Usage data told a short story: four got opened weekly. The other 42 were museums.

So we ran an experiment. For one quarter we froze every dashboard except the four, and we added one rule; if a leader asked for a new chart, they had to name the decision it would change.

Six requests came in. Two named a decision. We built those two.

Is it a perfect system? No. Some leaders still screenshot charts into slides and lose the filters (I do this too, when I'm rushed).

Still, the team got back roughly a day a week that used to go to maintenance. We spent it on the pipeline model instead.

Open your BI tool's usage log today. Sort by last viewed. Anything untouched in 90 days goes on a freeze list, and you tell people why.
