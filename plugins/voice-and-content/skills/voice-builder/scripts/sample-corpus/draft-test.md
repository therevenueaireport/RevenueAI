# Your QBR deck is too long

Last quarter I timed our QBR. The deck had 41 slides and we got through 17.

So this quarter we tried a rule: one slide per decision we need from the room. We had five decisions. We made five slides (plus an appendix nobody opened, which is fine).

Did we miss anything? One leader asked for the win-rate trend; it was in the appendix, slide 3. Took ten seconds.

The meeting ended 20 minutes early. Nobody complained.

Count the decisions you need from your next QBR. Build that many slides. Run the numbers afterward and see what you missed.
