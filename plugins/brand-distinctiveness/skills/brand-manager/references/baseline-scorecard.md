# Baseline scorecard: worked example and template

Part 1 is a fully worked baseline for a fictional brand, so you can see what a
finished read looks like. Part 2 is a blank template. Copy Part 2 into a new file per
brand you score.

---

## Part 1. Worked example: Acme Pipeline Lab (fictional)

**The brand.** Acme Pipeline Lab is a fictional training-and-community business for
revenue operations leaders. It sells a cohort course, runs a weekly podcast, and
publishes a newsletter. The founder hosts the podcast, writes the newsletter, and
keynotes the brand's two annual workshops. Before founding the company, the founder
spent ten years as a session drummer.

**Status.** DRAFT. The scores below are provisional. Run `audit-protocol.md` against
the live assets (site, podcast, newsletter, social, decks, ads) and replace every
"confirm" before treating this as a real audit. The strategic read does not depend on
the exact numbers and is the point of this file.

### Provisional scores

| # | Asset | Score (draft) | Status | Note |
|---|-------|---------------|--------|------|
| 1 | Color | 1/3 | confirm | A navy-and-white palette, the same as four of the five rival programs. Pick one owned hue and hold it across site, newsletter, deck, social. A single owned hue beats a palette. |
| 2 | Logo | 1/3 | confirm | A funnel icon next to a default-sans wordmark. Two rivals also use a funnel. Not theirs alone. |
| 3 | Visual device | 0/3 | confirm | Ad-hoc graphics per post, no recurring non-logo form. Likely an open lane. |
| 4 | Typography | 1/3 | confirm | The neutral sans every tool ships with. Easy to lift to a 2 with one display face. |
| 5 | Taglines | 1-2 | confirm | Two lines compete ("Build the machine" and "Ops is strategy"). Decide the ONE line that comes back everywhere and repeat it to a 2. |
| 6 | Human identity | 2, 3 possible | strong | The founder is a recurring, visible, vocal face across podcast, newsletter, workshops, and social. The strongest built asset and the easiest to push toward 3. |
| 7 | Product UI motif | n/a-low | confirm | Education and community, not a single software product. The course-module frame or a signature slide system serves as the stand-in surface. |
| 8 | Music / sound | 0 today, 3 possible | open goal | Empty today, like almost every B2B brand. See the unfair advantage below. |

Do not total these until the confirms are resolved. A total on guessed numbers is
theater.

### The strategic read

Two assets decide this brand, and both are wide open in the category.

**Human identity is already half-built. Finish it.** The founder is a real recurring
face across the podcast, the newsletter, the workshops, and social. Most B2B brands
score zero on this asset. The move is not to invent a mascot. It is to systematize the
face already in play: a consistent framing, a consistent role (for example, always
introduced as "the Lab's head of research"), the same photographic treatment and
backdrop everywhere, so the persona does the retrieval work. Consider whether a
lightweight recurring device (a mark or motif tied to that role) extends the human
asset without competing with it.

**Sound is the open goal, and it is uniquely theirs.** Sound is the emptiest asset in
B2B: published audits of B2B brands find almost every brand scores zero on it. It is
the highest-opportunity asset and the hardest to brief, which is exactly why nobody
has done it. This founder is a trained drummer. The single hardest distinctive asset
to build in B2B is the one this brand is most equipped to build, and no rival
program can easily follow. That is the definition of an asset a competitor cannot
comfortably copy.

Concrete surfaces for one consistent sonic device: the podcast intro and outro, a
short sting under the newsletter's video cutdowns, the walk-on at workshops and
talks, the open on webinars and course modules, and mid-rolls in other people's
podcasts. Build one motif, three to five seconds (for example, a four-hit snare
figure that resolves on a rim click), and hold it across all of them. Route to
[[brand-asset-sound]] to design it.

### Recommended next move

1. Resolve the confirms with a real audit pass.
2. Do not chase color and typography to feel "done." Lift them to a clean 2 and stop.
3. Commit the real investment to sound, and tighten human identity to a 3 via
   [[brand-asset-human-identity]].
4. Hold both across every surface for four quarters without a refresh. That is the
   whole play.
