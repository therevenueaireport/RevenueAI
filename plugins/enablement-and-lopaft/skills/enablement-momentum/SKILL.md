# Enablement Momentum — Mid-Program Motivation and Adoption Skill

Every program that runs longer than four weeks hits the same wall.

Week 1-2: Energy is high. The new thing is interesting. People show up.
Week 3-4: The novelty is gone. The skill is still effortful. The old habits are asserting themselves. Competing priorities are back. This is Conscious Competence — the hardest, most uncomfortable stage. And it's where most programs die.

This is not a people problem. It is a design problem. The mid-program energy dip is predictable. Which means it is preventable — if you build for it.

This skill gives you the architecture to sustain momentum through the hard middle and into genuine behavioral adoption.

This skill operates in two modes:
- **Design mode**: Build motivation sustainability into the program before launch
- **Rescue mode**: Diagnose a stalling program and intervene with precision

---

## Why Programs Stall: The Five Root Causes

Before intervening, identify which root cause is driving the stall. The fix is different for each.

| Root Cause | Symptoms | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| WHY erosion | Engagement scores dropping, pre-work not being done, "this doesn't apply to me" | Re-activation event — reconnect to original WHY with new evidence |
| Conscious Competence discomfort | "This is harder than I expected," reps avoiding practice, regression to old habits | Normalize the dip, celebrate effort not just outcome, reduce rep count not complexity |
| Environmental competition | Competing priorities, manager attention shifting, new initiative launched | Leadership reaffirmation, calendar protection, temporary scope reduction |
| Absent social proof | No visible wins yet, no peer models succeeding, isolation | Surface early wins, pair struggling learners with advancing learners |
| Coaching quality degradation | Manager coaching sessions becoming shorter, less frequent, less specific | Coach the Coach intervention (see enablement-coach) |

## Quick Stall Diagnostic

Run this when a program shows signs of stalling:

**Engagement signals:**
- [ ] Is attendance / pre-work completion dropping from Week 1-2 levels?
- [ ] Are engagement scores (relevance, participation) trending downward?
- [ ] Are learners' Q3 open-text responses ("what will you do differently") becoming shorter or more vague?

**Motivation signals:**
- [ ] Are learners expressing that the skill "doesn't apply" or "isn't working"?
- [ ] Is self-reported confidence dropping or flattening when it should be rising?
- [ ] Are managers reporting that reps are avoiding practice or reverting to old habits?

**Environmental signals:**
- [ ] Has a competing initiative, sprint, or priority been introduced since launch?
- [ ] Has manager coaching cadence dropped below the designed frequency?
- [ ] Has executive visibility on the program decreased since launch?

**Social proof signals:**
- [ ] Has at least one early win been surfaced and shared with the cohort?
- [ ] Is there a visible example of a peer succeeding with the skill?
- [ ] Are learners talking about the skill outside of formal program sessions?

**Scoring**: If 6+ items are checked, a stall is either in progress or imminent. Intervene now. Do not wait for lagging metrics to confirm it.

## Proactive Momentum Design — The 5 Non-Negotiables

For any program longer than 4 weeks, these must be built in at design time:

1. **Early win architecture** — design a moment in weeks 2-3 where learners can succeed at a simplified version of the skill and experience the win
2. **WHY re-activation event** — a planned touchpoint at week 4 that reconnects learners to the original motivation with new evidence
3. **Social proof system** — a mechanism for surfacing and sharing peer wins throughout the program
4. **Executive visibility cadence** — leadership acknowledgment of the program at weeks 1, 5, and end
5. **Competing priority protocol** — a pre-designed decision framework for what happens when other initiatives compete for the cohort's time

---

## Audit Mode Checklist — Momentum Architecture Assessment

Use when evaluating an existing program's motivation sustainability:

- [ ] Was a mid-program WHY re-activation event designed into the calendar?
- [ ] Is there an early win mechanism in weeks 2-3?
- [ ] Is there a social proof sharing system (wins channel, share-out, cohort board)?
- [ ] Is executive visibility structured at defined program milestones?
- [ ] Is there a protocol for competing priorities?
- [ ] Are engagement leading indicators (attendance trend, pre-work trend, confidence trend) being tracked weekly?
- [ ] Is there a designed intervention trigger (what metric + threshold causes a stall intervention)?

**Scoring**: 6-7 checked = strong momentum architecture. 4-5 = vulnerable to mid-program stall. Below 4 = stall is likely; proactive intervention needed.
