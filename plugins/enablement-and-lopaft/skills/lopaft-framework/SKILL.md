# LOPAFT Framework — Master Orchestrator

LOPAFT framework by Jonathan Kvarfordt. He developed this integrated pedagogical system through 500+ coaching engagements, GTM enablement work, and AI education. It is validated by elite performance science, adult learning research, and real-world revenue outcomes. This skill applies it to program and workflow design; for definitions and explaining the framework, see lopaft.

**Two systems. One engine.**
- The **Four Levels of Competency** = diagnostic map (WHERE is the learner?)
- **LOPAFT** = operational cycle (HOW do you move them?)

---

## The Four Levels of Competency

| Level | Name | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unconscious Incompetence | Don't know what you don't know | 2-year-old in the backseat, no idea dad is driving |
| 2 | Conscious Incompetence | Aware of the gap, can't close it yet | 10-year-old realizing they can't drive |
| 3 | Conscious Competence | Can do it — but must actively think | 15-year-old, hands at 10 and 2, full focus |
| 4 | Unconscious Competence | Skill is instinct, no cognitive load | 25-year-old doing makeup, eating, driving with a knee |

**The critical failure**: Most training tries to jump from Level 1 → Level 3 via an information dump. It skips Level 2 (creating genuine awareness of the gap) and the deliberate practice mechanics that move Level 3 → Level 4. That is why adoption fails. LOPAFT closes that gap systematically.

**Getting someone to Level 2 is itself a teaching act.** You cannot motivate someone to close a gap they do not feel. Surfacing the gap without shame is the first job.

## LOPAFT — The 6-Step Competency Cycle

**L — Learn | O — Observe | P — Practice | A — Application | F — Feedback | T — Teach**

LOPAFT is a spiral, not a line. Each pass through the cycle advances the learner's baseline. What was Application at one level of complexity becomes Practice at the next. The cycle repeats as skills scaffold upward.

| Step | Core Function | Moves Learner |
|---|---|---|
| L — Learn | Knowledge transfer + WHY activation | L1 → L2 |
| O — Observe | Model good and bad execution | L2 → early L3 |
| P — Practice | Safe, deliberate reps | L2 → L3 |
| A — Application | Real-world deployment | L3 → early L4 |
| F — Feedback | Calibration + spaced repetition | L3 → L4 |
| T — Teach | Creation, retrieval, gap detection | L3 → L4 (deepest encoding) |

## Traffic Light — When to Deploy LOPAFT

| Light | Change Type | Example | Required Intervention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | Informational — no behavior change | Salesforce field renamed | Slack message, email, Loom |
| Yellow | Process adjustment — workflow shift | Updated call framework | Multi-channel comms + light reinforcement |
| Red | Behavior change — new skill required | New sales motion, AI adoption | Full LOPAFT cycle, spaced repetition, verified |

**Decision test**: Does this require someone to do their job differently on a call, in a meeting, or in a document? Red light. Full cycle.

**Most common failure**: deploying a red light change with a green light intervention.

## Quick Diagnostic

| Symptom | Likely Gap | Step to strengthen |
|---|---|---|
| "I don't know where to start" | L1 → L2 transition | L (Learn) |
| "I get it but I can't do it" | Missing O step | O (Observe) |
| "They practice but don't improve" | Bad practice design or missing F | P (Practice) + F (Feedback) |
| "Works in drills, not real calls" | Missing A | A (Apply) |
| "They plateau and stop growing" | Missing T or F spacing | F (Feedback) + T (Teach) |
| "Zero adoption after training" | Missing O + P + F entirely | O + P + F together |

## Companion Audit Skills

LOPAFT designs the program. These four skills instrument, deliver, and sustain it. Use them at the phases noted.

| Skill | When to Deploy | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| `enablement-assess` | BEFORE design — establish learner baseline and level distribution | Proactive design OR retroactive audit |
| `enablement-measure` | AT design time — build leading + lagging metrics into the program | Proactive design OR instrumentation audit |
| `enablement-coach` | BEFORE launch — assess and develop manager coaching capability | Proactive design OR coaching quality audit |
| `enablement-momentum` | AT design time (6+ week programs) AND if stall detected mid-program | Proactive design OR rescue intervention |

**Full program quality audit**: Load all four companion skills and run their audit checklists against the program. A program that passes all four audits is as close to guaranteed success as program design can get.
