# GTM AI Playbook Library

These 166 playbooks are sequenced AI workflows for go-to-market teams, each with an owner, tool, pitfalls, definition of done and KPIs, and each mapped to an L1-L6 AI maturity level (L1 Starter, L2 Assisted, L3 Integrated, L4 Orchestrated, L5 Autonomous, L6 Rebuilt). The full playbooks are free on [therevenueaireport.com](https://www.therevenueaireport.com/playbooks).

**Teams:** [Customer Success](#customer-success) (19) · [Enablement](#enablement) (18) · [General](#general) (18) · [Marketing](#marketing) (24) · [Org Design](#org-design) (19) · [RevOps](#revops) (32) · [Sales](#sales) (36)
